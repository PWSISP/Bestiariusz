# S04-02a — Fan of cards in the hand

File to edit: src/view/hand-fan.ts

Goal: the combat screen draws the hand (up to 5 cards) as a fan at the bottom of the screen; this module computes where each card is, how it is rotated, how big it is and in which order the cards are drawn. The selected card rises above the fan.

Signatures (do not change): the constants, the `FanSlot` interface and `fanLayout` in the stub. `Rect` comes from `src/view/geometry.ts`.

Rules (`area` is the rectangle of the hand, `count` the number of cards, `selectedIndex` the selected card or null):
1. `count` must be an integer >= 0, otherwise throw a `RangeError`. `count` 0 returns `[]`.
2. For card `i` let `offset = i - (count - 1) / 2` (negative on the left, 0 in the middle).
3. `step` = 0 when `count` is 1, otherwise `min(MAX_STEP, (area.width - 2 * SIDE_MARGIN - CARD_WIDTH) / (count - 1))`, and never below 0.
4. `x` = the horizontal middle of the area (`area.x + area.width / 2`) + `offset * step`.
5. The resting `y` of the middle of the fan is `restY = area.y + area.height - BOTTOM_GAP - CARD_HEIGHT / 2`. A card that is not selected has `y = restY + ARC_DROP * offset * offset`.
6. A card that is not selected has `rotation = offset * ROTATION_PER_SLOT`, limited to the range -MAX_ROTATION … MAX_ROTATION, `scale` 1 and `z` equal to its index.
7. The selected card (`selectedIndex` an integer from 0 to `count - 1`) has the same `x` as without selection, `y = restY - LIFT` (the arc is not added), `rotation` 0, `scale` SELECTED_SCALE and `z` = `count` (above all others). The other cards do not change.
8. A `selectedIndex` of null, below 0, not below `count` or not an integer means "no selection".
9. Never modify the arguments. Return new objects.

Edge cases:
- 1 card in an area 360 wide: x is the middle of the area, rotation 0, y = restY.
- 5 cards in an area 360 wide: step is 63, so the x offsets from the middle are -126, -63, 0, 63, 126.
- 2 cards in an area 360 wide: step is 69 (MAX_STEP), x offsets -34.5 and 34.5, rotations -0.025 and 0.025.
- an area too narrow for the cards (width below SIDE_MARGIN * 2 + CARD_WIDTH): step 0, all cards at the middle.
- 11 cards: the outer rotations are limited to ±0.2.
